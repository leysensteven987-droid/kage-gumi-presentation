聴 Chō capture

Privacy policy

Chō capture is a Chrome extension that records a meeting you are attending in a browser tab, together with your own microphone, and streams that audio to your Kage-gumi server for live transcription and a written summary. It is a companion to that server and does nothing without it.

What the extension sends, and where

Everything the extension sends goes to one place: the Kage-gumi server at https://kg.kage-gumi.com, using your existing login on that server. It sends nothing to the developer, to analytics services or to any other third party.

DataWhen
Meeting audio from the tab you start a capture onOnly while a capture you started is running, uploaded in short chunks
Your microphone audio, as a separate streamOnly while a capture is running, and only if you allowed microphone access
The display name you entered in the popupOnce, when a capture starts, so your spoken lines can be labelled with it
How participants were told they are being recorded (the disclosure you pick)Once, when a capture starts

While the popup is open it also reads back the latest transcript lines from the same server, to show you what has been heard.

What the extension stores

  • Your display name, in Chrome sync storage, so it follows your Chrome profile.
  • The server address, in Chrome local storage.

The extension keeps no recordings, transcripts, summaries or history of its own. Capture status (levels, upload receipts) lives in memory only and is gone when the capture ends.

What happens on the server

The Kage-gumi server is operated by its owner, not by this extension. On that server the audio is transcribed locally, and the transcript and summary are saved there as meeting records. The summary is written by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude), which receives the transcript text for that purpose. Audio chunks are deleted from the server once transcribed.

When it runs

  • A capture never starts on its own — only when you press Start in the popup.
  • Every capture requires you to state how the participants were informed; the server refuses a capture without it.
  • Chrome shows its own tab-capture indicator for as long as the tab is being recorded, and the extension's toolbar icon shows a REC badge.

Permissions

  • tabCapture — record the audio of the tab you start a capture on.
  • offscreen — hold the audio streams in a background document for the length of the capture.
  • storage — keep your display name and the server address.
  • activeTab — act only on the tab you are on when you press Start.
  • Host access to kg.kage-gumi.com — the one server the audio is sent to.
  • Microphone — requested once through the extension's own permission page; optional.

Your choices

Decline microphone access to capture the tab only. Change or clear your display name under Settings in the popup. Removing the extension deletes its stored settings. Meeting records held on the Kage-gumi server are managed by that server's owner.
